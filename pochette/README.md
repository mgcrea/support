# Pochette Support

Pochette edits the tags, names and folders of a music library kept as files, on Mac, iPhone
and iPad. There is no import and no database: open a folder and it is your library. Every
change is shown as a plan before it is made, and Undo takes it back.

## The quickest route: the feedback form

**[Send feedback](https://pochette.mgcrea.io/feedback/)**. No GitHub account, and nothing is
posted publicly. An email address is optional; leave it blank and the report is anonymous.

Opening it from the app's Help menu fills in your Pochette version, OS version, device model
and language. All four are visible and editable on the page before you send, and the app
itself never transmits anything: it hands your browser a web address and stops.

## Open an issue here

If you would rather have a public thread others can follow, [open an
issue](https://github.com/mgcrea/support/issues/new?labels=pochette&title=%5Bpochette%5D+).
Please include your Pochette version, your device and OS version, where the folder lives
(this device, iCloud Drive or an external drive), the file's format, and what you did,
expected, and saw. A screenshot of the plan sheet helps when a plan looked wrong. Please
don't attach music you don't own the rights to share.

## Everything else

The [support page](https://pochette.mgcrea.io/support/) has the FAQ and the troubleshooting
steps.

For anything you would rather not post publicly, email
[support@mgcrea.io](mailto:support@mgcrea.io).
