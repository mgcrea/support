# Balise Support

Balise puts French IGN topographic maps and your GPX tracks on your Mac, iPhone and iPad, and works without a connection.

## The quickest route: the feedback form

**[Send feedback](https://balise.mgcrea.io/commentaires/)** — no GitHub account, and nothing is posted
publicly. An email address is optional; leave it blank and the report is anonymous.

Opening it from inside the app (**Help → Send Feedback**) fills in your Balise
version, OS version, device model and language. All four are visible and editable on
the page before you send, and the app itself never transmits anything — it hands your
browser a web address and stops.

## Open an issue here

If you would rather have a public thread others can follow, [open an
issue](https://github.com/mgcrea/support/issues/new?labels=balise) and it will be
labelled `balise`. Please include your Balise version, your OS version, and what you
did, expected, and saw.

## Everything else

The [support page](https://balise.mgcrea.io/support/) has the FAQ, the system requirements and the
troubleshooting steps. It is kept up to date with the app; this file deliberately does
not repeat it, so the two cannot drift apart.

For anything you would rather not post publicly, email
[support@mgcrea.io](mailto:support@mgcrea.io).
