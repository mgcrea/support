# Canopy Support

Canopy keeps a record of the plants and trees in your garden: what each one is, what it
needs, when to cut it, and what to do when it looks sick. The data lives in your own
iCloud — there is no account, and everything except identifying a photograph works
offline.

## The quickest route: the feedback form

**[Send feedback](https://canopy.mgcrea.io/feedback/)** — no GitHub account, and nothing is
posted publicly. An email address is optional; leave it blank and the report is anonymous.

Opening it from inside the app fills in your Canopy version, OS version, device model and
language. All four are visible and editable on the page before you send, and the app
itself never transmits anything — it hands your browser a web address and stops.

## Open an issue here

If you would rather have a public thread others can follow, [open an
issue](https://github.com/mgcrea/support/issues/new?labels=canopy) and it will be labelled
`canopy`. Please include your Canopy version, your OS version, and what you did, expected,
and saw.

## Everything else

The [support page](https://canopy.mgcrea.io/support) has the FAQ, the system requirements
and the troubleshooting steps.

For anything you would rather not post publicly, email
[support@mgcrea.io](mailto:support@mgcrea.io).
